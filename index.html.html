Problem Statement

Disaster Risk & Hydrodynamic Challenge

Dam breaks, uncontrolled reservoir releases, and glacial-lake outburst floods (GLOF) are catastrophic hydrological events. Without physics-driven inundation simulations, response teams operate blindly.

!

The Problem

Floods caused by dam breaches, gate releases, and glacial outbursts strike rapidly and are notoriously difficult to predict. Disaster rescue agencies (NDRF, SDRF, Army) lack accurate predictions of flood spread, water depth, velocity, and village-wise arrival time. Delayed evacuations cause tragic loss of life and infrastructure collapse.

✓

The Solution: VARUNA

VARUNA predicts floods from dam breaches, heavy gate discharges, and glacial-lake outbursts using coupled hydrodynamic modeling. Simulating flood extent, water depth, flow velocity, and arrival times, it identifies high-risk villages, plots safe shelter routes, and feeds 4 real-time role-based command dashboards.

Innovation

Core Technological Pillars

A modern, open-source pipeline that turns satellite telemetry into immediate on-ground rescue decisions.

01

Dual Hydro Engine

Couples Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (DualSPHysics) for turbulent 3D near-field breach mechanics with Delft3D-FM for basin-wide 2D shallow-water flood routing.

02

Automated Data Fetching

Auto-ingests Copernicus/SRTM DEM, Sentinel-1 SAR (penetrating heavy monsoon clouds), Sentinel-2, Landsat, and live CWC river discharge data without manual upload bottlenecks.

03

4 Role-Based Dashboards

Tailored situational interfaces for Technical Analysts (NDMA/CWC), Field Responders (NDRF/SDRF), Local Administration (Collectors/Panchayats), and Public Civilians.

04

Citizen SOS Channel

Direct alert link from civilians trapped in danger zones transmitting real-time GPS locations and timestamps directly to active HADR boat rescue teams.

Feasibility & Viability

Buildable Today, Scalable Tomorrow

Built on proven open-source physics solvers and freely available satellite telemetry.

Technical Feasibility

  • Zero Proprietary Licensing: Built on open-source Delft3D-FM, DualSPHysics, and GDAL.
  • Public Telemetry: Ingests freely accessible Copernicus/SRTM DEM and Sentinel-1/2 SAR.
  • Proven Physics: Employs Saint-Venant shallow water equations used by leading global hydrologic agencies.
  • Production Architecture: High-performance FastAPI backend with PostGIS spatial database.

Operational Feasibility

  • Automated Polling: Background cron workers continuously monitor satellite radar and river gauges.
  • Agency Alignment: Directly maps to standard operating procedures (SOPs) of NDMA, NDRF, and SDMAs.
  • Containerized Rollout: Docker orchestration allows instant deployment on cloud servers or mobile field vans.
  • Recurring Utility: Monsoon floods recur annually in India, guaranteeing continuous operational value.

Challenges Overcome

  • Monsoon Cloud Cover: Sentinel-1 SAR Synthetic Aperture Radar pierces through heavy rain clouds.
  • Near-Field Turbulence: SPH particle solver resolves violent 3D surges smoothly where 2D models crash.
  • High-Speed Compute: Hybrid architecture couples SPH only at breach site with fast 2D routing downstream.
  • Frontline Connectivity: Offline-first caching enables rescue tablets to navigate without active cell towers.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

Dual-model physical accuracy (SPH + Delft3D-FM), 100% automated open data ingestion, and 4 role-based command views.

Weaknesses

Dense 3D particle simulation in SPH requires GPU compute during scenario generation; mitigated by pre-computed deterministic libraries.

Opportunities

Nationwide scaling across 5,300+ large Indian dams and dangerous Himalayan glacial lakes; native integration with national NDMA Sachet alerts.

Threats

Satellite constellation revisit latency during rapid cloudbursts; actively mitigated through Sentinel-1 SAR radar fusion and gauge interpolation.
